# Helpbook

**Connecting Those Who Want to Help With Those Who Need Help**

Saved 2026-09-21 as written by Taylor. This is the mission and direction for the project; every design and development decision should be checked against it. Change it only when Taylor changes it.

## Mission

Helpbook is a community-impact 501(c)(3) organization designed to connect people, businesses, foundations, nonprofits, schools, workforce development programs, and other eligible tax-exempt organizations around meaningful opportunities to help.

At the center of Helpbook is the concept of Golden Threads — the connection points between community needs and the people, organizations, funding, skills, and resources capable of addressing them.

Helpbook exists to strengthen those connections and turn them into community impact.

## The Opportunity

Communities have enormous amounts of untapped capacity.

Nonprofits need volunteers, funding, expertise, equipment, mentorship, and resources. Schools need business partnerships and community support. Workforce development programs need employers and professional connections. Individuals want meaningful ways to contribute. Businesses want to engage their employees and communities. Foundations want to support effective organizations and initiatives.

Yet these resources and needs are often disconnected.

Helpbook creates a trusted community-impact network that makes those connections easier to discover.

Need → Match → Golden Thread → Action → Impact

## The Golden Threads

Helpbook organizes opportunities around the connections that can create meaningful community benefit:

- **Volunteer Thread:** Individuals and businesses → nonprofits and community organizations
- **Skills & Expertise Thread:** Professionals → organizations needing specialized expertise
- **Donation & Resource Thread:** Businesses and individuals → organizations needing resources
- **Corporate Partnership Thread:** Businesses → nonprofits and community initiatives
- **Education Thread:** Businesses, professionals, and nonprofits → schools and school districts
- **Workforce Thread:** Employers and professionals → workforce development programs and participants
- **Grant Discovery Thread:** Foundations and grantmakers → validated organizations seeking funding
- **Community Impact Thread:** Multiple organizations and community resources → shared community initiatives

Each connection represents a Golden Thread between a need and someone capable of helping.

## Grant Discovery

Helpbook would provide grant discovery, not grant-writing or grant-submission services.

Only validated users and eligible organizations would have access to grant-search functionality.

Helpbook could identify and organize publicly available or authorized grant opportunities according to factors such as:

- Mission
- Geographic area
- Population served
- Program area
- Eligibility
- Funding priorities
- Grant size
- Application deadlines

When an organization identifies an opportunity, Helpbook would direct it to the appropriate foundation, funder, Benevity, or other authorized platform for the actual application process.

Helpbook discovers the opportunity.
The authorized platform handles the application.

Helpbook would not generate or submit grant applications.

## Trust & Validation

Because Helpbook operates as a community-impact organization, trust is fundamental.

The platform can establish verification processes for:

- 501(c)(3) organizations
- Other eligible tax-exempt organizations
- School districts and educational organizations
- Workforce development organizations
- Businesses
- Foundations
- Authorized organizational representatives

Grant-search functionality would be restricted to validated users.

This helps create a trusted environment while reducing fraudulent organizations, spam, and misuse.

## Businesses & Individuals

Businesses and individuals can participate without needing to navigate dozens of separate organizations and websites.

They can discover opportunities based on:

- Location
- Interests
- Skills
- Available time
- Professional expertise
- Community priorities
- Volunteer opportunities
- Donation needs
- Educational opportunities
- Workforce-development initiatives

Businesses can also identify opportunities for employee volunteering, skills-based service, charitable support, mentorship, and community partnerships.

## Corporate & Technology Integrations

Helpbook would complement existing charitable-giving and employee-engagement ecosystems.

The organization could pursue integrations with platforms such as Benevity and other appropriate systems, subject to available APIs, agreements, and privacy requirements.

Potential integrations could make Helpbook opportunities discoverable through existing corporate programs while allowing those systems to continue handling their specialized functions.

Helpbook's role is the community connection layer.

## 501(c)(3) Community Impact Model

As a 501(c)(3), Helpbook's primary purpose would be community benefit rather than private commercial profit.

Potential charitable activities could include:

- Connecting volunteers with community organizations
- Expanding access to community resources
- Supporting nonprofit capacity
- Connecting students with community and career resources
- Connecting workforce programs with employers and mentors
- Increasing awareness of charitable opportunities
- Facilitating access to grant information
- Developing community partnerships
- Conducting educational and community-impact initiatives
- Supporting underserved communities
- Building technology that advances these charitable purposes

Core services for eligible community organizations could remain free or highly accessible.

Funding could come from charitable contributions, foundation grants, corporate sponsorships, mission-aligned partnerships, and other revenue sources permitted within the organization's charitable structure.

## Vision

Helpbook makes it easier for everyone to find a meaningful way to help.

Our vision is a community where a person with time, a professional with expertise, a business with resources, a foundation with funding, or an organization with a community need can find the right connection.

Helpbook doesn't simply create a directory.

It creates connections.

It creates Golden Threads.

And those Golden Threads create community impact.
