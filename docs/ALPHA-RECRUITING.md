# Finding the first organizations for the Helpbook alpha

Draft for Mario, 2026-09-23. Taylor approves before it goes out.

## What Helpbook is, in two sentences

Seattle nonprofits post what they need and what they have extra, and find each other. When two of them help each other, it shows up as a golden thread on a map of the city.

## Who we're looking for

- **10 or so nonprofits in urban Seattle.** Capitol Hill, the Central District, Ballard, Fremont, the U District, Beacon Hill, Rainier Valley, SODO, downtown.
- **Groups that would trade with each other.** A food bank, a tool library, a tech refurbisher, a youth program, a shelter, a mutual aid group. Three or four near each other beats ten spread out.
- **One person at each** who answers email and can decide to give or take something small. Up to three staff or volunteers from the same group can join.
- They have to be a 501(c)(3) or another tax-exempt group. We look them up on the IRS list, so they don't fill in forms.

## What you can tell them

**What it costs:** nothing. No money moves through Helpbook.

**What we ask:**
1. 20 minutes to sign in and post one thing you need and one thing you could give.
2. Check your inbox in the app twice a week, for October.
3. Two 15-minute calls with Taylor, mid-October and at the end.

**What they get:** a first look, a say in what gets built, and a real shot at getting something they need from a neighbor.

**Be honest that it's early.** It's a first version. Some things will be rough, and we'll fix what they tell us about. Only the other alpha organizations see what they post. Nothing is public.

**How they get in:** they need a Google account to sign in. A personal one is fine. Taylor sends each organization an invite code.

## What to send Taylor

For each organization that says yes, send:

- The organization's name
- The contact person's name and email
- The neighborhood they're in
- One thing they need right now, if they mentioned it

Taylor sends the invite and the code. Please don't forward invite codes; each one is tied to one organization.

## Questions they might ask

**Is this a company?** No. It's being set up as a nonprofit. The plan is to keep it free for community organizations.

**Who sees what we post?** Only the other organizations in the alpha.

**What about our data?** We keep your name, your email, what you post, and your messages. Nothing else. Ask Taylor and it's deleted.

**Can we post a funding need?** Not in the alpha. Money stays out. Grants come later, as a way to find funders.

**Do we have to use a Google account?** For now, yes. A personal one works. Other sign-ins come later.
