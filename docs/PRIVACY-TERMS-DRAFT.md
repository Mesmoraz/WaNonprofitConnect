## Page: /privacy

### Headline: What we keep, and why

Helpbook helps Washington nonprofits trade what they have for what they need. To do that we keep a little about you. Here is all of it.

**What we keep**

- Your name and email, from your Google sign-in. Google also gives us an account number, so we know it's you next time.
- Which organization you're with, and how you joined: an invite code, or a claim we approved.
- What your organization posts: needs, offers, and the details you write.
- Your threads: who started them, your messages, and whether they're done.
- How often each post has been seen, as a number. Not who saw it.
- Your organization's filters, such as who they serve.
- Errors. If a screen breaks on your device, we get the page, the error, and your browser's name. Not your messages or anything you typed.
- Feedback you send. Your note, and a picture of your screen if you leave that ticked, with the page you were on, your device and browser, and recent errors. Only the people who run Helpbook see it.

**What we don't do**

- We don't sell or rent anything about you. To anyone.
- We don't show ads, and we don't track you across other sites.
- We don't handle money. No donations, payments or card numbers go through Helpbook.

**Who sees what**

- Everyone signed in can see your organization's name, city and posts, and your organization on the map.
- Messages in a thread are seen by the two organizations in it. The people who run Helpbook can see them too, but only look when we have to keep the site safe or fix a problem.
- Your email is never shown to other organizations.

**Where it lives**

- On Microsoft Azure servers in the western United States. Azure backs it up and keeps backups for 7 days.
- Google handles sign-in. When you open the map, OpenFreeMap sends the map tiles, and Google Fonts sends the lettering. Those services see your device's internet address, as any website does.

**Cookies**

- One cookie, to keep you signed in. It lasts 30 days. No tracking cookies.

**Public information we use**

- The IRS list of tax-exempt organizations, and Census Bureau data, to place organizations on the map. This is public, and it's how your organization was already here before you joined.

**Your choices**

- To see what we keep about you, or to have it deleted, email **[contact email]**. We'll answer within **[7]** days.
- When we delete your account, your name and email go. Your organization's posts stay unless you ask us to remove those too.

**Changes**

- If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Last updated **[date]**. Questions: **[contact email]**.

### Headline: How Helpbook works

These are the rules for using Helpbook. By signing in, you agree to them.

**Who runs it**

- Helpbook is run by **[Taylor Philpott, until the nonprofit is formed / the organization's legal name]**. It's free for nonprofits.
- This is an early version, shared with a small group. It will change, and sometimes it will break. Tell us when it does.

**Who can join**

- You must be 18 or older and work or volunteer for the organization you join as.
- Only join as an organization you're allowed to speak for.

**What you post**

- Post real needs and offers, for your organization.
- No money: don't ask for or offer cash, donations or payments here.
- Nothing illegal, unsafe, hateful, or meant to mislead.
- You keep what you write. You let us show it in Helpbook so others can respond.
- We may remove a post or an account that breaks these rules. We'll tell you why.

**Between organizations**

- Helpbook helps you find each other. What you agree to, the things you hand over, and any pickups or deliveries are between your organizations.
- We don't check the condition of items, and we aren't part of your agreement. Use the same care you would with any partner.

- We work to keep Helpbook running and your information safe, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of mistakes.
