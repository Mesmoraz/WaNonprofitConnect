# Helpbook app map

Approved 2026-09-21 (round A2). What the prototype is made of after the revamp. Public-facing copy says "Name TBD" until Taylor says otherwise.

## Tabs

Five. Grants shows only for confirmed organizations; "Just me" sees four.

| Tab | What it's for |
|---|---|
| Board | Needs and offers, fairly ranked. Home. Directory search sits at the top ("Search everyone here"). |
| Map | Threads and golden threads across Washington. |
| + | Post a need or an offer. |
| Grants | Search grants; apply on the funder's site. Confirmed organizations only. |
| Inbox | Your threads and alerts. |

Your profile is behind your name in the top bar. No Orgs tab, no Me tab.

## Deep vs. shown

| Kind of help | In the prototype |
|---|---|
| Time, Skills, Things, Partnership, For schools, Jobs and mentoring | Built. One shared flow (post, board, thread, golden) with kind of help as a field. Seeded examples of each. |
| Grants | Built. Own screen with the mission's eight filters. Seeded grants from real WA foundations' public pages, labeled as examples. |
| Together | Shown. One seeded initiative on the board and as a multi-ended thread on the map. Tapping it explains it. No flow. |

## The story

Three short stories; the guide offers them as a choice. Each ends on the map.

| Story | Who | Kind of help | Steps |
|---|---|---|---|
| Casey needs laptops | Nonprofit to nonprofit | Things | 5: sign up, post, Jordan answers, accept, done |

| Casey finds a grant | Nonprofit to foundation | Grants | 3: open Grants, filter, apply on the funder's site |

## On the design page, not in the app

The mission in full, the six participant types and how each is confirmed, the eight threads, why money stays off, why Grants is one-directional, integrations (Benevity and the like) as a later phase, the 501(c)(3) model and funding, the flywheel as a diagram, what Together will be.
