## Things to know

- Today is between cycles for most Washington community foundations. Only N2N (G-1) has an upcoming deadline, and its status line says the summer cycle is paused and the fall reopening is expected, not confirmed.
- The Innovia county list (which 10 Eastern Washington counties) was not found on a page that loaded; the JSON says "Eastern Washington (10 counties) and North Idaho (10 counties)" exactly as the apply page does.
- EINs in the JSON were supplied by the task brief, not read from the funders' pages.
- The CFSPS Community Grants page still reads "Total awards in 2025"; the "opens again in 2027" line is the current-status statement.
