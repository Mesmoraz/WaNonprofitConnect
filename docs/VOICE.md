# Name TBD voice sheet

Approved 2026-09-21. Every screen gets checked against this before it is built.
Living copy (comments, edits): https://claude.ai/code/artifact/6fd2dc58-f52f-410e-a0ec-2f3948ff2d7a

The app is one person at a nonprofit writing to another. Not a company, not a product.

## Rules

1. **Talk to one person.** "You" and "your". "We" only when we are promising something ("We'll tell you when a match shows up"). Never "users", "members", "the platform".
2. **One idea per sentence.** Headline: 6 words or fewer. Body under a headline: 2 sentences at most. A third sentence belongs on the help sheet or nowhere.
3. **Say what happens next, not how it works.** "Urgent needs first. Then whoever's been seen least." Not the ranking rules.
4. **One name per thing.** See Words below. The same thing is never a match on one screen and a connection on the next.
5. **No notes to ourselves in the app.** Lines like "verification methods are pluggable" or "decision 6 in the brief" come out or move to the design doc. Demo scaffolding that has to stay gets one form: a small grey line starting with "Demo:", as in "Demo: verification is instant here."
6. **Buttons are the verb.** Post need. Accept. Send. Mark done. Not Continue, OK, Submit, Go. One green button per screen, pinned to the bottom, and it is the thing you came to do.
7. **Punctuation.** Sentence case everywhere, buttons and tabs included. No em-dashes: a period or a comma instead. No ellipses, no exclamation marks. Middle dots only in metadata lines (Seattle · Food · 1989).
8. **Numbers are digits.** 2 orgs, 12 laptops, 3 mi. Dates as Sep 21, never 2026-09-08. Money rounded: $17M, not $17,321,514.
9. **Plain words.** Banned: leverage, seamless, empower, platform, solution, pluggable, equitable, fulfilled, verified. Say fair, done, confirmed.
10. **Empty states and errors are a sentence and a next step.** "Nothing here with those filters. Clear them?" where "Clear them" is the link. Not "Nothing matches these filters."

## Words

One name per thing. The left column is the only word for it in the app.

| Use | Not |
| --- | --- |
| organization (org only in tight labels like the tab); means any of the five organization types in TAXONOMY.md | nonprofit as a synonym; 501(c)(3) except where the legal term matters |
| need, offer | listing, except for the pair: "your needs and offers" |
| thread (a need and an offer, matched, in progress) | connection, match, hand-off |
| golden thread (a thread that is done) | completed connection, fulfilled |
| start a thread (the verb) | propose, connect |
| kind of help (Time, Skills, Things, Partnership, For schools, Jobs and mentoring, Grants, Together) | category |
| people (individuals); everyone here (all six kinds) | supporters, community members, users, members, participants |
| message | reply, note |
| Urgent, This week (and nothing at all for the two lower levels) | P0 to P3, Critical, High, Normal, Low |
| done | fulfilled, completed |
| confirmed (or just the check mark) | verified |
| fair | equitable |
