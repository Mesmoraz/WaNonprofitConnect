## 2026-10-25 · A calmer map: solid pins, one row of controls, a shorter legend (S-147)

**Story S-147,** built to the brief in docs/ALPHA-BRIEFS.md ("A calmer map"), approved by Taylor in chat Oct 25. Taylor, from staging on a phone: too many threads and moving pieces, too much clutter at the top, and the green, orange and white symbols with numbers aren't clear.

### What changed

- **One organization, one solid dot.** Amber if it has needs, green if it only has offers, a little bigger with more open, never a number. Nothing open: a small white pin with a grey edge. The split amber and green ring is gone.
- **Only clusters carry numbers.** A cluster keeps its ring around how many organizations it holds. Lines no longer carry numbers.
- **The threads still move.** Tried still threads on staging; Taylor brought the drifting dashes, sparks and glow back ("the animated threads really added something"). The grey cluster bubbles tried in the same round went back to the ringed ones.
- **One row at the top.** The count, then **Filter**, then the ?. Filter opens the kind-of-help chips; picking one closes them and the button says which ("Filter: Things").
- **The two buttons at the bottom stay** ("Offers for your needs", "Needs you can help with"). A single What fits you button was tried on staging and dropped.
- **A shorter ?:** Needs help. Offers help. Several organizations close together (a ringed bubble with a number). Help that was given. Your threads that are underway. Faint dots are Washington nonprofits that haven't joined yet.
- **Home's small map and the list of who's in a place** use the same symbols.
