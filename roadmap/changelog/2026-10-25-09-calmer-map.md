## 2026-10-25 · A calmer map: one meaning per symbol, still threads, one row of controls (S-147)

**Story S-147,** built to the brief in docs/ALPHA-BRIEFS.md ("A calmer map"), approved by Taylor in chat Oct 25. Taylor, from staging on a phone: too many threads and moving pieces, too much clutter at the top, and the green, orange and white symbols with numbers aren't clear.

### What changed

- **One organization, one solid dot.** Amber if it has needs, green if it only has offers, a little bigger with more open, never a number. Nothing open: a small white pin with a grey edge. The split amber and green ring is gone.
- **Grey bubbles mean "several organizations here".** The number on a bubble always counts organizations. Lines no longer carry numbers.
- **The threads are still.** Golden threads are thin, solid gold lines: no sparks, no drifting dashes, no glow. Tap a place and only its threads light up and glow; the rest fade. Your own threads underway stay faint and dashed.
- **One row at the top.** The count, then **Filter**, then the ?. Filter opens the kind-of-help chips; picking one closes them and the button says which ("Filter: Things").
- **One button at the bottom: What fits you.** It opens "Offers for your needs" and "Needs you can help with", or goes straight to the one you have.
- **A shorter ?:** Needs help. Offers help. Several organizations close together. Help that was given. Your threads that are underway. Faint dots are Washington nonprofits that haven't joined yet.
- **Home's small map and the list of who's in a place** use the same symbols.
