## 2026-10-27 · Editing a post's count no longer reads "2 unit" (S-135)

### What changed

- **A unit written for one is dropped when the count goes to many, and the other way round.** An older post that said "1 unit", edited to 2, now reads "2 needed" (or "2 to give"), the line every new post uses. A unit that still fits stays: "12 laptops" edited to 15 reads "15 laptops".
- Only older posts have a unit (the seed and the CLI); the Post screen stopped asking "Of what" in an earlier round. No new words: "needed" and "to give" are the lines already on the board.
