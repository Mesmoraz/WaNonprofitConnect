## 2026-10-20 · Edit a need or offer (S-97), and the rest of S-109

**Stories S-97 and S-109,** Round 3 of `docs/ALPHA-PLAN.md`. Brief: "Edit a need or offer (S-97)" and "Small lines (the rest of S-109)" in `docs/ALPHA-BRIEFS.md`, approved by merging #28.

### What changed

- **Your own post** that's still on the board has a plain button **Edit this need** / **Edit this offer**, above the red close button. Not on a closed or done post.
- It opens Post filled in, headed "Edit your need" / "Edit your offer", without the "We need" / "We have" chips: a need stays a need. Everything else can change. **Save changes**, then "Saved." and back to the post.
- With threads open on it, above the button: "1 thread is open on this. They'll see the change." The other organization sees the new words in its thread.
- An edited post reads "Seattle · Posted 2 days ago · Edited".
- **Urgent counts from when a need became urgent** (T-235), so editing a post to Urgent counts like posting one. Editing an urgent post keeps its place, and taking Urgent off doesn't give it back.
- **Why Urgent is greyed out:** once you've used both, the hint reads "You've used Urgent twice this month. You can use it again on Oct 21." with the real date.
- **On an offer,** the message box says "Say what you'd use it for and when." (it said "Say what you can do and when.", which fits a need).
- API: `PUT /api/v1/listings/{id}`; `editedAt` on every post, `openThreads` on your own; `urgentAgainOn` on Me. New columns `Listings.EditedAt` and `UrgentSince`, with urgent needs already posted counting from when they were posted. Contract in `docs/ALPHA.md`. The demo does the same.
