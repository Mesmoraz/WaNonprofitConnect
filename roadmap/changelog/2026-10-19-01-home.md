## 2026-10-19 · Home: a calm first tab that points to the board, the map, and what's waiting (S-126)

**Story S-126,** built to the brief Taylor approved on Oct 19 (docs/ALPHA-BRIEFS.md, "Home"). Taylor asked for a calm place to land that leads to the board, the map, and what's waiting. After seeing it on staging, Taylor asked for Near you to be a small live map in place of the still picture ("I'd rather it truly be a live map"), and approved that brief the same day.

### What changed

- **Home is the first tab.** Tabs are now Home, Board, Map, Post, Inbox, and the logo goes to Home. The Board moves to its own address, `/board`. A link opened before signing in still lands where it pointed.
- **Your organization:** your name, then your city and cause ("Olympia · Food"), then one line like "2 needs, 1 offer open · 3 golden threads · Your page". Until you have an open need and an open offer, the welcome card shows there instead, with its approved words. It no longer sits on top of the Board.
- **Waiting on you:** up to 3 threads where the next step is yours, each saying "{Org} started a thread" or "1 new message". Tap one to open it. With none: "Nothing waiting on you."
- **Near you:** a small live map around your organization, about 25 mi each way: your pin, every Washington nonprofit as a faint dot, organizations here as pins, and their threads, moving as on the Map. It stays put on Home and loads after the rest of Home, with a plain drawing of the same place until then. Under it: "12 organizations, 2 golden threads within 25 mi. Open the map". Tap it and the Map opens on the same place.
- **New this week:** the 3 newest needs and offers from the last 7 days that pass your "Who they serve" filter, with "See the whole board". With none: "Nothing new this week. See the whole board?"
- **On the board:** up to 5 rows that open the Board already filtered, each saying what it is with the count the Board will show: "4 needs you could meet", "78 posts from food organizations" (your cause), "78 posts under Things" (the busiest kind of help), "2 urgent needs", "12 posts that match your filters". A row with nothing behind it doesn't show. Taylor found the first version's chips ("Go to: Food · 78") unclear on staging.
- **No green button on Home**, as approved. The welcome card's two buttons are plain here too.
- **Roadmap:** a task under S-113 records that local test runs need `BASE_URL` set, or every test that opens a second phone fails.
